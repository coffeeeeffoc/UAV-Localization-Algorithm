# Literature and source provenance

Start with [research-notes.md](research-notes.md). The source registry is [references.json](references.json), with a [CSV review table](references.csv) and the manuscript [BibTeX bibliography](../paper/references.bib).

Every source has a verification level and explicit reading boundary. Metadata-only entries are reading candidates; their titles do not establish detailed scientific claims. [proposal-reference-audit.md](proposal-reference-audit.md) records the limited audit of the supplied proposal. [access-log.json](access-log.json) retains retrieval evidence, including a failed DOI lookup that was corrected by a title search. Selected metadata is archived under `metadata/`; third-party full texts and datasets are not redistributed.

This is a targeted initial review dated 2026-10-23. It is not an exhaustive literature search, a completed real-data study, or an independent reproduction of the cited papers.
